My story

I'm a senior at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, graduating in December 2026 with a degree in Journalism and Political Science. I grew up in Scottsbluff, in the Nebraska Panhandle, and I've been chasing stories ever since: the ones that shape our communities and the ones that rarely get told.

Politics and journalism are two halves of the same job for me. At Nebraska Nightly I anchor live newscasts, interview newsmakers and, as executive producer, help lead the newsroom. In the Daily Nebraskan I write opinion columns and edit and fact-check my peers' work. And on my own Punctual Politics podcast, I break down the week in political news.

In summer 2025 I was selected as one of 21 students nationwide for the Carnegie-Knight News21 fellowship in Phoenix, an investigative reporting project on housing in America. I spoke with more than 100 people across 10 cities and produced photo essays profiling people who have faced homelessness.

When I'm not in the newsroom, I'm often on the campaign trail, in the classroom as a college ambassador teaching workshops to high schoolers, or in the Legislature taking notes on hearings. I like being close to the people and the process I report on.
